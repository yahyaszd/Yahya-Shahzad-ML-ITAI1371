# Final Project - Hotel Reviews

## What I Did
In this final project, I worked on a comprehensive machine learning assignment focused on analyzing hotel reviews. I worked through a Jupyter Notebook (`FP_HotelReviews_YahyaShahzad.ipynb`) where I applied various data processing and modeling techniques learned throughout the course to an extensive real-world dataset.

## What I Learned
- **End-to-End Workflow:** I learned how to integrate multiple stages of the machine learning pipeline—from exploratory data analysis and preprocessing to model training and evaluation—into one cohesive project.
- **Handling Real-World Data:** I improved my ability to handle messy, real-world textual data (like hotel reviews) and extract meaningful features from it.
- **Model Selection & Evaluation:** I practically applied my knowledge to test different models and used robust evaluation metrics to identify the most effective solution.

## Challenges Faced
- **Data Complexity:** The dataset was extensive and required careful preprocessing to ensure the models could learn effectively without getting bogged down by noise.
- **Hyperparameter Tuning:** Fine-tuning the models to squeeze out the best performance required significant computational time and thoughtful parameter selection.
- **Synthesizing Concepts:** Bringing together all the disparate concepts from individual labs into one robust final project required careful planning and a deep understanding of how each piece fit into the larger workflow.
